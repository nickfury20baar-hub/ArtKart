The Complete ArtKart Guide: Buy, Sell & Explore Original Art

Written by ArtKart Team  |  Last Updated: March 2026  |  Reading Time: 12 mins

ArtKart is India's growing online art marketplace where real artists sell original paintings, sketches, digital art, and handmade creations directly to collectors and art lovers. This comprehensive guide covers every feature of the platform — from creating your account and browsing artworks to uploading your own art and managing orders. Whether you're a first-time buyer or an experienced artist looking to sell, this page has everything you need.

1. What is ArtKart?

ArtKart (https://art-kart.netlify.app) is a modern, mobile-first art marketplace designed specifically for the Indian art community. Unlike generic e-commerce platforms, ArtKart is built from the ground up to serve the unique needs of artists and art buyers. Every artwork listed on the platform is an original creation uploaded directly by the artist — there are no mass-produced prints, no dropshipping, and no middlemen taking large commissions.

The platform supports multiple art categories including oil paintings, acrylic works, watercolor pieces, pencil and charcoal sketches, digital illustrations, and traditional Indian art forms like Warli and Madhubani. Artists can create detailed profile pages that serve as their online portfolio, while buyers get access to a curated feed of authentic artwork with transparent pricing and secure delivery across India.

ArtKart operates on a simple principle: artists should keep control of their work and their earnings, and buyers should have confidence in what they're purchasing. The platform verifies artist identities during registration, reviews listings for quality, and provides a structured support system for any issues related to orders, delivery, or refunds.

2. Creating Your Account

Getting started on ArtKart takes less than a minute. You can register as a buyer (art lover) or as an artist (seller). Both account types are free to create. The registration process collects your basic information — name, email, and a password. If you're signing up as an artist, you'll also set up your artist profile with a bio and display picture, which becomes your public portfolio page on the platform.

Your account gives you access to several features: liking and saving artworks to your wishlist, following your favorite artists, placing orders with secure payments, and (if you're an artist) uploading and managing your artwork listings. All your activity — orders, likes, uploads — is accessible from your profile page.

Step-by-Step Registration:

  1. Go to the Sign Up page from the header button or direct link (register.html).
  2. Enter your full name, email address, and create a strong password.
  3. If registering as an artist, fill in your artist name, a short bio about your art style, and upload a profile picture.
  4. Click Create Account. You'll be signed in automatically.
  5. You'll land on the Home page where you can immediately start browsing artworks or, if you're an artist, head to the Upload page to list your first piece.

3. Browsing & Exploring Artworks

The Explore page is where you discover art on ArtKart. It displays all uploaded artworks in a clean, image-first grid layout. Each artwork card shows the piece's image, title, artist name, price in Indian Rupees, and a like count. You can scroll through the entire collection or use the search functionality to find specific pieces by title, artist name, or style.

The Home page also features a "Most Liked" section that surfaces the most popular artworks based on community engagement. This is a great starting point if you're new to the platform and want to see what other collectors are appreciating. Tapping on any artwork card takes you to the detailed artwork page where you can see a larger image, full description, and the artist's profile link.

How to Find the Right Artwork:

  • Start with Most Liked: The Home page's "Most Liked" section shows community-favorite pieces. This is great for discovering quality art.
  • Browse Explore: Visit the Explore page to see all artworks. Scroll through the grid and tap anything that catches your eye.
  • Check Artist Profiles: Click on the artist's name to visit their profile page. You'll see their full portfolio and can follow them for updates.
  • Use the Like Button: Tap the heart icon on any artwork to like it. Liked artworks help surface popular content for other buyers.
  • Review Details: Always open the artwork detail page before purchasing. Check the dimensions, medium, and any description the artist has provided.

4. Buying Artwork on ArtKart

Purchasing original art on ArtKart is a straightforward process designed to be transparent and secure. When you find an artwork you want, you click through to its detail page, review the information provided by the artist (including price, medium, dimensions, and any additional notes), and proceed to place your order. The platform supports multiple Indian payment methods to make the process convenient.

Once your order is placed, the artist receives a notification and begins preparing your artwork for shipment. Since every piece is original and often created on-demand or carefully stored, the artist handles the packaging personally to ensure it arrives in perfect condition. You receive tracking information once the artwork has been shipped, and delivery typically takes 5-10 business days depending on your location within India.

Step-by-Step Buying Process:

  1. Find Your Artwork: Browse the Home or Explore page and tap on an artwork card to view its full details.
  2. Review the Details: On the artwork page, check the image carefully, read the title, artist name, price, and any description about the medium or dimensions.
  3. Check the Artist: Tap the artist's name to view their profile. Look at their other works and bio to understand their style and credibility.
  4. Place the Order: Select "Buy Now" or "Add to Cart" on the artwork page. Choose your payment method — UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit/debit card, or net banking.
  5. Complete Payment: All transactions are processed through secure, encrypted payment gateways. You'll receive an order confirmation.
  6. Track Delivery: The artist packages and ships your artwork with tracking. Monitor your order status and expect delivery within 5-10 business days.

5. Uploading & Selling Your Art

If you're an artist, ArtKart gives you a free platform to reach buyers across India without the need for a personal website, social media marketing, or gallery representation. The upload process is designed to be quick — you add a photo of your artwork, fill in the details, set your price, and publish. There are no approval wait times or follower count requirements. If you create art, you can sell it on ArtKart.

Your artist profile serves as your portfolio page. When buyers click on your name from any artwork, they see your full collection, bio, and profile picture. This means every artwork you upload contributes to building your visible presence on the platform. Over time, as you accumulate likes and sales, your profile becomes a powerful showcase of your artistic journey.

How to Upload Your First Artwork:

  1. Make sure you're logged into your artist account.
  2. Navigate to the Upload page using the quick action button on Home or your profile menu.
  3. Click the image upload area and select a high-quality photo of your artwork. Use good lighting and a clean background for the best presentation.
  4. Enter the Title (e.g., "Sunset Over Jaipur"), your Artist Name, and a Price in Rupees.
  5. Add a description mentioning the medium (oil, acrylic, watercolor, pencil, etc.), dimensions, and any story behind the piece.
  6. Click Upload. Your artwork is now live on the Explore page and visible to all ArtKart users.
  7. Share the link to your artwork or profile on social media to drive more views and potential buyers.

6. Payments, Delivery & Refunds

ArtKart supports a wide range of Indian payment methods to ensure every buyer can pay comfortably. UPI payments through Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm are the most popular option due to their instant confirmation. Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay) and net banking are also supported. All payment processing is handled through encrypted, secure gateways — your financial information is never stored on ArtKart's servers.

For delivery, each artist is responsible for carefully packaging the artwork to prevent damage during transit. Paintings are typically wrapped in protective layers, rolled or flat-packed depending on size, and shipped through tracked courier services. You receive a tracking link once the shipment is dispatched. Standard delivery takes 5-10 business days across India.

If the artwork you receive differs significantly from what was listed (wrong color, damaged, different piece entirely), ArtKart's refund policy covers you. Contact the support team through the Contact page within 48 hours of delivery with photographs of the received artwork and the issue. The team will review your case and coordinate with the artist for a resolution, which may include a replacement or full refund.

Payment & Refund Summary:

  • Accepted Methods: UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, Wallets
  • Security: All transactions SSL-encrypted through trusted payment gateways
  • Delivery Time: 5-10 business days across India with tracking
  • Packaging: Done by the artist with protective materials
  • Refund Window: Contact support within 48 hours of delivery with photos
  • Refund Coverage: Significant discrepancies between listing and received artwork

7. Artist Profiles & Portfolios

Every artist on ArtKart gets a dedicated profile page that functions as their online portfolio. This page displays the artist's name, profile picture, bio, and a complete grid of all their uploaded artworks. It's a powerful tool for building your brand as an artist — you can share your ArtKart profile link anywhere (Instagram bio, WhatsApp status, LinkedIn) and potential buyers can browse your entire collection in one place.

The profile page also shows your total number of artworks, giving visitors a sense of your productivity and range. As you upload more pieces and receive likes, your profile naturally becomes more attractive to buyers. There's no algorithm hiding your work — every artwork you upload is immediately visible on the Explore page and on your profile, giving you equal opportunity regardless of your follower count or how long you've been on the platform.

8. Categories of Art on ArtKart

ArtKart hosts a diverse range of art forms. The primary categories include Paintings (oil, acrylic, watercolor, and mixed media works on canvas or paper), Sketches (pencil drawings, charcoal sketches, pen and ink illustrations), Digital Art (illustrations, concept art, and digital paintings offered as prints), and Handmade Art (traditional Indian art forms like Warli, Madhubani, and other folk and tribal art styles).

Each category attracts a different type of buyer. Paintings tend to be the most popular for home decor and gifting. Sketches are often purchased by collectors who appreciate raw artistic skill. Digital art appeals to a younger, design-conscious audience. Handmade and traditional art forms have a dedicated following among buyers looking for culturally significant pieces. As an artist, understanding which category your work falls into helps you price it appropriately and write descriptions that attract the right audience.

9. Tips for Artists Getting Started

If you're new to selling art online, here are practical tips to make the most of ArtKart. First, invest time in photographing your artwork well. Use natural daylight, a clean background (a plain wall or white sheet works), and take multiple angles. The image is the first thing buyers see — a blurry or poorly lit photo will lose you sales regardless of how good the actual artwork is.

Second, write meaningful titles and descriptions. Instead of "Painting 1," use something like "Golden Hour at Marine Drive — Oil on Canvas, 18x24 inches." Include the medium, dimensions, and a brief story about what inspired the piece. Buyers connect with stories, and detailed descriptions build trust.

Third, price your work fairly. Research what similar artworks on ArtKart are priced at, consider your material costs and time invested, and set a price that reflects your skill level while remaining accessible. As you build a reputation and receive positive engagement, you can gradually increase your prices.

Fourth, be consistent. Upload regularly — even one new piece per week keeps your profile active and gives returning visitors something new to see. Share your ArtKart profile link on your social media accounts, WhatsApp groups, and with friends and family. Every share is a potential sale.

10. Safety, Trust & Community

ArtKart takes trust seriously. Every artist goes through a registration process that verifies their identity. Artwork listings are reviewed to ensure they represent original creations. The platform does not allow mass-produced prints sold as originals, stolen artwork, or misleading descriptions. If you ever encounter a listing that seems inauthentic, you can report it through the Contact page and the team will investigate.

For buyers, the combination of verified artists, secure payments, tracked delivery, and a clear refund policy creates a safe purchasing environment. For artists, the platform provides direct access to buyers without gallery commissions or platform fees eating into earnings. The community aspect — likes, artist profiles, and the ability to discover new talent — makes ArtKart more than just a marketplace; it's a growing ecosystem for Indian art.

Key Trust Features:

  • Verified Artists: Identity verification during registration ensures real people behind every listing
  • Original Artwork Only: No mass-produced prints or dropshipped items allowed on the platform
  • Secure Payments: SSL-encrypted transactions through established payment gateways
  • Tracked Delivery: Every shipment comes with tracking information for full visibility
  • Refund Protection: 48-hour window to report issues with photo evidence for resolution
  • Direct Communication: Buyers can reach artists and support directly for transparency
