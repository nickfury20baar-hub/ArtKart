What is ArtKart?

We believe every artist deserves to be seen, and every art lover deserves access to authentic, affordable artwork. Whether you're a seasoned collector or buying your first piece, ArtKart makes the experience simple and transparent.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using ArtKart, from buying to selling to shipping.

Is ArtKart free to use? expand_more

Yes, creating an account and browsing artworks is completely free. For artists, listing your artwork is also free. We only charge a small platform fee when a sale is successfully completed.

How do I know the artwork is original? expand_more

Every artwork on ArtKart is uploaded directly by the artist. We verify artist identities during registration and review listings for quality. Each piece comes with the artist's profile, so you can see who created it and contact them directly if needed.

What if I don't like the artwork after receiving it? expand_more

We understand that buying art online can feel uncertain. ArtKart has a refund policy that covers significant discrepancies between the listing and the received artwork. Contact our support team within 48 hours of delivery with photos, and we'll help resolve it.

How does delivery work? expand_more

Once you purchase an artwork, the artist is notified and packages the piece carefully. Shipping is tracked and typically delivered within 5-10 business days depending on your location. You'll receive tracking details via email and can monitor your order status.

Can I become a seller on ArtKart? expand_more

Absolutely! Anyone who creates original art can become a seller. Simply register, set up your artist profile, and start uploading your work. There's no approval process or minimum follower requirement — if you make art, you can sell it on ArtKart.

What payment methods are accepted? expand_more

We support UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit and debit cards, net banking, and popular wallets. All payments are processed through secure, encrypted gateways to ensure your financial information stays safe.
